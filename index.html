GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is AudioKit free?

Yes. This starter version runs in your browser and does not require an account or a paid conversion service.

Are my audio files uploaded?

No. The conversion code processes audio in your browser. The MP3 encoder library is loaded from a public CDN, but your selected audio file is not sent to that CDN or to an AudioKit server.

Does converting MP3 to WAV improve sound quality?

No. WAV can be useful for compatibility or editing, but converting a lossy MP3 to WAV does not restore audio information that was lost during MP3 encoding.

Why won't some audio files open?

Input decoding depends on the browser and operating system. Some codecs and formats, including some FLAC or OGG files, may not be supported in every browser.
