MP3 TO WAV FAQ

About MP3 to WAV conversion

Why convert MP3 to WAV?

WAV files are commonly used in editing workflows and some audio applications. Converting an MP3 to WAV may improve compatibility, but the resulting file is typically larger.

Will WAV improve MP3 sound quality?

No. WAV is usually uncompressed, but converting a lossy MP3 to WAV cannot restore audio information that was lost during MP3 encoding.

What should I keep in mind?

Keep your original MP3. A WAV copy can be helpful for compatibility, but it does not become a lossless original just because the file extension changes.
